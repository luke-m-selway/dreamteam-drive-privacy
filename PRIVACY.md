# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 15 November 2026**

Dream Team Drive rclone is a private, personal-use integration used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive account from owner-controlled computers.

## Google user data

The integration requests Google Drive access only after the account owner explicitly authorizes it through Google OAuth. That access is used solely to perform file operations requested by the owner, such as listing, uploading, and downloading files.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored only on owner-controlled machines. They are not published in this repository.

## Data sharing and use

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or offered to third parties. The integration is not available for third-party accounts or public sign-up.

Google user data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or model training. Data is processed only as needed to carry out the owner's requested Google Drive operations.

## Data retention

This integration does not operate a separate hosted database of Google user data. Files remain in the owner's Google Drive or on owner-controlled machines according to the owner's actions and normal Google Drive settings.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke the integration's Google account access at any time through Google Account security settings.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the repository owner through their GitHub account.
